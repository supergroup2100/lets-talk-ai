🌊 Change, or Go Under!

課文互動問答 • Global Warming / Panama / Maldives • Reading Skill: Problem and Solution

📖 課文重點回顧

Global warming is leading to a rise in sea levels around the world. This is putting people's lives in danger. However, people in some places are fixing these problems now.

🇵🇦 Panama – Gardi Sugdub Island
People said goodbye to their homes and moved away. They built a new town far away from the sea, and many fishermen became farmers. They didn't just sit and cry about climate change. They changed, and they are living new lives now.

🇲🇻 The Maldives – Floating City
People are designing a new city. It can rise and fall with sea levels. People can build safe houses there. This city can also protect sea life. It can work with nature and may never go under!

🇹🇼 Taiwan
Taiwan is an island, too. We should learn from the examples above and also make some changes, or we may go under!
