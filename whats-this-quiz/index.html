🎙️ What's This?

Dialogue 互動問答 • Game Show: Judy / David / Amy • Warm-up: What is your favorite TV show?

📖 對話重點回顧

Show 1 - goose
Judy: Welcome to What's This? I am Judy.
Hello, David and Amy. Are you ready for the game?
David & Amy: Yes!
Judy: Look at this picture. What is this?
Amy: It's a duck.
Judy: No, it isn't.
David: It's a goose.
Judy: Yes, it is. Ten points for you!
Show 2 - tigers
Judy: Look at this picture. What are these?
David: Those are lions.
Judy: Sorry. They are not lions.
Amy: They are tigers!
Judy: You are right! You are a smart cookie! Ten points for you!
Show 3 - Bigfoot
Judy: And, what's this?
David: It's a monkey!
Judy: I'm sorry, no.
Amy: Is it big and tall?
Judy: Yes, it is.
Amy: It's Bigfoot!
Judy: That's it! Ten more points! You are the winner!
show (n.) 節目
picture (n.) 圖片
goose (n.) 鵝；複數 geese
point (n.) 分數
lion (n.) 獅子
monkey (n.) 猴子
more (adj.) 更多的
winner (n.) 贏家
smart cookie 聰明的人
Bigfoot 大腳怪
