💬 The Dialogue

Ken: The GPS says to turn left here.

Betty: It's taking us a faster way!

Ken: The GPS knows that because it's using AI, right?

Betty: Yes! When we were driving, the GPS helped us avoid traffic.

Ken: AI is a big help if you use it the right way.

GPS 使用 AI 來避開車流、找更快路線。AI 要用對方法才有幫助！

💡 Tips 1 & 2

① Give Clear & Specific Information

Tell AI: what you want, why you want it, who it's for.

給 AI 清楚具體的資訊：你要什麼、為什麼、給誰用

✏️ "Write a formal email to my boss about a meeting."

② Give Examples

Examples help AI understand your requests better.

給 AI 範例，讓它更懂你的需求

✏️ "Like this email: 'Dear Mr. Chen…'"

💡 Tips 3 & 4

③ Break Big Tasks into Smaller Parts

AI works better when you give it one small task at a time.

把大任務切成小步驟，AI 更容易處理

✏️ First: "List 3 ideas." → Then: "Write about idea 1."

④ Give Helpful Feedback

Tell AI: "Too casual." or "Make it shorter."

給 AI 回饋，告訴它哪裡需要改

✏️ "This is too casual. Make it more formal."

💡 Tip 5: Try a Few Times!

"It may take a few tries before AI finally understands your request."

Don't give up! Keep giving feedback.

不要放棄！多試幾次，給 AI 更多回饋，它就會給你你想要的

📌 The key is: be patient and specific

📝 Grammar Tip: Do vs. Write

❌ "I don't want to write my homework." ✗
✅ "I don't want to do my homework." ✓

In English, we say "do homework", not "write homework".

英文中「做功課」要用 do homework，不是 write

📌 You write words, but you do homework.

🗣️ Calendar Phrase

"It's taking us a faster way."

Use this when something (like GPS) finds a quicker route.

當某個東西幫你找到更快的方法或路線時使用

🗣️ "The GPS says to turn left. It's taking us a faster way!"

🗣️ "This new app is taking us a faster way to learn English."
