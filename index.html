The Core Insight

"People follow leaders based on how good those leaders make people feel about their future."

It's not about a detailed strategic plan — it's about giving people a sense that tomorrow will be better.

人們追隨領導者，是因為領導者讓他們對未來感到更好

The Myth of Vision & Charisma

Ask people why they follow leaders, and you'll often hear vision and charisma.

But most leaders don't have both. Many don't have either.

What they do have are a few super skills that are disproportionately strong.

迷思：大多數人認為領導者需要願景和魅力，但作者發現多數領導者兩者皆無

Super Skills

One or two strengths so powerful that they convince people:

✨ "The future will be different and better."

Don't

Waste time trying to fit some generic leadership model

不要試圖符合通用領導模板

Do

Figure out the 1-2 strengths that make people optimistic & lean into them

找出你的超級技能，並全力發展它

Who Has Super Skills?

John Levy's dinner guests — leaders from every field:

🎬 Oscar-winning directors → filmmaking

🎵 Grammy-winning artists → music

🏅 Olympic athletes → physical excellence

✊ Martin Luther King → hope & vision

📻 Dr. Doris Broom (Studio Classroom) → pioneer in English education

Their super skill drew people — not a detailed strategic road map.

各領域頂尖人士都擁有自己的超級技能，吸引人們追隨

The Other Half: Team Intelligence

"Leadership explains why people gather. Team intelligence determines whether they succeed."

Team intelligence is not about IQ, degrees, or resumes.

It's about the habits and skills that make groups smarter together than they could ever be alone.

團隊智商 ≠ 個人智商；是讓團隊一起比單獨更聰明的習慣與技能

The Basketball Lesson

Studies show: it isn't the players with the highest salaries or raw talent who decide the outcome.

It's the quality of the coach.

The coach aligns reasoning, manages attention, and makes sure resources are used well.

籃球研究：決定勝負的不是明星球員，而是教練的品質

Three Pillars of Team Intelligence

🧠 Reasoning

Aligning on clear goals & purpose

Debates → better solutions, not power struggles

釐清目標與目的

👁️ Attention

Managing focus & communication

Psychological safety — people share ideas freely

心理安全感

🔧 Resources

Surfacing hidden skills & networks

Right expertise at the right time

發掘隱藏技能與人脈

Psychological Safety

The story of The Emperor's New Clothes:

Everyone was afraid to speak the truth — until a child said, "You're not wearing any clothes!"

Without psychological safety, leaders become like the emperor — nobody dares to say what's really going on.

沒有心理安全感，團隊就變成「國王的新衣」——沒有人敢說真話

What the Coach Does

✅ Aligns reasoning on goals

✅ Manages attention and focus

✅ Surfaces hidden skills

✅ Ensures resources are used well

✅ Cultivates team intelligence

The best coaches may not be the best players — they just know how to make the team greater than the sum of its parts.

好教練不一定要是最強選手，但要能讓團隊發揮綜效

Final Advice for Leaders

🚫 Stop

Chasing the illusion of being well-rounded

不要追求十項全能

✅ Start

Recognize your super skill — the thing that makes people feel tomorrow will be better

找到你的超級技能

Then focus on cultivating team intelligence — when reasoning, attention, and resources are in place, your team doesn't just follow — they succeed.

Key Vocabulary

myth — a widely held but false belief 迷思

super skill — a disproportionately strong ability 超級技能

lean into — emphasize and develop 全力投入

generic — not special, the same as others 通用的

well-rounded — good at everything 全方位

align — organize into correct relationship 對齊

cultivate — develop and improve 培養

surface — bring to the surface 浮現、發掘

Remember

Find one thing you do better than virtually anyone else.

And lean into it.

— from City Slickers (1991) to John Levy's research
